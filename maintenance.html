HVAC Service & Maintenance Programs

Preventative maintenance is the key to keeping your HVAC equipment running smoothly long-term — extending its life, cutting energy costs, and preventing breakdowns.

Why It Matters

Preventative Maintenance Pays for Itself

Every property has unique heating and cooling needs, but no matter the system, regular service is the single best way to protect your investment. It helps:

  • Extend the life of your equipment
  • Prevent costly, inconvenient breakdowns
  • Maintain peak energy efficiency & lower bills
  • Ensure year-round comfort in Florida's climate

By scheduling seasonal tune-ups in spring and fall, you avoid unexpected breakdowns during the hottest and coldest months — when you need your system most.

Residential Comfort Club

Our preventative maintenance program includes a routine inspection performed by certified technicians on a yearly or bi-annual basis. Members receive 15% off repair parts (up to $250 per visit).

Every inspection includes:

  • Voltage & amperage check
  • Refrigerant pressures
  • Evaporator air temp
  • Air filters
  • Motor mounts & belts
  • Thermostat calibration
  • Flush drain lines
  • Electrical connections
  • Contactors & capacitors
  • Subcool & superheat
  • Lubricate motors
  • Coil inspection & rinse
